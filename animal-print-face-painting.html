Animal Print Face Painters Across Sussex & Surrey

Animal print is one of the most requested face painting styles I offer — bold, striking and endlessly versatile. Whether it's a fierce tiger look for a themed birthday party, elegant gold leopard print for a hen do, or a delicate butterfly design for a festival, animal print face painting suits children's parties and adult events alike across Sussex, Surrey and Brighton.
